DESKTOP FEATURES

Find your flow.

Your desktop player has more to offer than a play button.

Queues, discovery and playlists

Explore tracks, albums and artists; organize the queue and playlists with the supported catalog.

Downloads and offline music

On supported desktop builds, download eligible tracks and collections, then play saved tracks without fetching them again.

Audio and lyrics

Tune playback with supported equalizer or transition controls and enjoy synchronized lyrics when available.

CONNECTED LISTENING

Optional integrations.

Depending on the desktop build, you can use Discord presence, Last.fm or ListenBrainz scrobbling and compatible Listen Together sessions.

Listen Together synchronizes playback state and controls; it does not host or redistribute audio. Each listener must have access to a playable source. Features and audio quality vary by platform.
