Music, on your terms.

Discover, collect and play music with vyon. Feature support can differ by platform, source, format and release.

DISCOVER & ORGANIZE

Your music collection, your way.

vyon combines music discovery with library and queue controls.

Search and discovery

Search for tracks, albums, artists and playlists from the available music catalog.

Library and playlists

Keep favorites organized, manage playlists and reorder your queue where supported.

Local music

Play music stored on your device; Android also includes SMB and WebDAV music-source integrations.

DOWNLOADS & OFFLINE PLAYBACK

Listen even without a connection.

Supported Android and desktop builds include downloads for tracks and collections; offline listening works with tracks already stored on the device.

Individual songs

Choose tracks for download instead of saving an entire collection.

Playlists and albums

Queue supported playlists or albums for download, with source-dependent availability.

Download management

Review queued transfers, quality options and progress where supported.

SOUND & DISPLAY

Make playback your own.

Adjust the experience without assuming the same controls exist on every device.

Equalizer and crossfade

Use supported equalizer presets and transitions for a more personal listening experience.

Lyrics and artwork

View synced lyrics, translations and animated artwork when corresponding data is available.

Audio quality

Choose supported streaming settings; lossless or Dolby Atmos paths require compatible sources, devices and decoders.
