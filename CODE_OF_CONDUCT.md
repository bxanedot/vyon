## ✨ The vibe we're building

> **Be curious. Be constructive. Be human.**

Vyon welcomes people of every background, identity, ability, and experience level. You don't need to be an expert to make a meaningful contribution.

Our community runs on a few simple principles:

| Principle | What it looks like |
| :-- | :-- |
| **🤝 Respect first** | Treat people with dignity, especially when you disagree. |
| **💡 Assume good intent** | Ask questions and clarify before jumping to conclusions. |
| **🎨 Make room for creativity** | Different ideas, styles, and approaches can make Vyon stronger. |
| **🧠 Feedback over ego** | Challenge ideas and code, not the person behind them. |
| **🌱 Help people grow** | Welcome newcomers, share context, and explain your reasoning. |
| **🔐 Protect trust** | Respect privacy, personal boundaries, and responsible disclosure. |

Disagreement is normal. Disrespect isn't.

---

## ✅ Do more of this

- **Be welcoming.** Help new contributors find their footing, even if their question seems obvious to you.
- **Keep criticism useful.** Explain *what* could improve and *why*. Suggest alternatives when possible.
- **Stay on topic.** Make issues, pull requests, and reviews easy for everyone to follow.
- **Give credit.** Acknowledge inspiration, contributors, and other people's work.
- **Respect boundaries.** If someone asks you to stop contacting them or to move on from a discussion, respect that.
- **Own mistakes.** Correct misinformation, apologize when appropriate, and learn from feedback.
- **Disclose security issues responsibly.** Follow our [security policy](SECURITY.md) rather than posting exploit details publicly.

**A helpful review sounds like:**  
> “I think this might break on smaller screens. Could we test it on Android TV too?”

**Not like:**  
> “This is terrible. How did you even write this?”

You can be direct without being cruel.

## 🚫 Not welcome here

The following behavior is unacceptable in Vyon project spaces:

- **Harassment, discrimination, or hate speech**, including slurs or derogatory comments about someone's identity or background.
- **Personal attacks, insults, threats, intimidation, or targeted bullying**, including repeated unwelcome attention.
- **Sexual harassment or unwanted sexual content or advances.**
- **Doxxing or privacy violations**, including sharing private messages, personal details, credentials, or identifying information without permission.
- **Trolling and deliberate disruption**, including flame wars, spam, coordinated pile-ons, or repeatedly derailing discussions.
- **Bad-faith contributions**, such as knowingly misleading reports, impersonation, malicious pull requests, or attempts to compromise users or the project.
- **Retaliation** against people who report concerns or participate in a good-faith review of an incident.

“It was a joke” does not excuse conduct that harms or intimidates others.

Constructive criticism of project decisions, technical approaches, and maintainer work is welcome. **Critique the work, not people's worth.**

## 🌍 Where this applies

This Code of Conduct applies to **Vyon-managed project spaces**, including:

- Repository issues, pull requests, code reviews, and discussions.
- Official project communication and collaboration channels, where available.
- Events or interactions where someone is speaking or acting on behalf of Vyon.

It is not intended to govern unrelated private conversations, but conduct that poses a credible threat to the safety of this community may be considered when moderating project participation.

## 🚨 Something happened? Tell us.

If you experience or witness conduct that violates this policy, **please report it privately whenever possible**.

1. Check the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/bxanedot) for an appropriate private contact method.
2. Include a brief description of what happened, the relevant links, approximate dates, and any evidence you can safely share.
3. If no private channel is available, you may [open a GitHub issue](https://github.com/bxanedot/vyon/issues/new) **solely to request a private contact route**. Do not publicly post sensitive allegations, personal information, private screenshots, or details that could expose someone to harm.
4. For abuse that violates GitHub's own rules, you can also use [GitHub's abuse reporting resources](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/github-acceptable-use-policies).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Your privacy matters.** Reports should only be shared with people reasonably necessary to review and resolve the concern. Complete confidentiality and specific response times cannot be guaranteed.

If you are in immediate danger, contact appropriate local emergency services. Project maintainers are not an emergency response service.

### What happens next?

Maintainers will aim to review reports fairly, gather context where practical, and choose a proportionate response. Reports will be assessed based on the behavior and available evidence, **not someone's popularity or contributor status**.

We do not promise a fixed response deadline, and we may be unable to share every detail of an outcome to protect privacy.

---

## ⚖️ How we handle violations

Depending on the severity, pattern, and context of an incident, maintainers may:

| Response | When it may be appropriate |
| :-- | :-- |
| **💬 Conversation** | Clarify expectations or ask for a change in tone or behavior. |
| **⚠️ Formal warning** | Explain a violation and set boundaries for future participation. |
| **⏳ Temporary restriction** | Limit participation after repeated or significant misconduct. |
| **🚪 Removal or ban** | Remove content or access for serious, threatening, or persistent violations. |

Serious incidents may warrant immediate action without earlier warnings. Where practical, people affected by moderation can request a review through a private contact route.

Maintainers may also edit, hide, or remove comments and contributions that violate this policy. These actions are intended to protect a productive community, **not to silence respectful disagreement**.

## 💌 A note to contributors

Every bug report, translation, accessibility improvement, design idea, and thoughtful review counts.

You don't have to agree with every decision. You don't have to love every feature. Just help make this a place where people feel comfortable building something together.

**Make cool things. Give credit. Leave the community better than you found it.**
